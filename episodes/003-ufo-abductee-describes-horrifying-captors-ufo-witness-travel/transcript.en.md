## Summary

- Former FBI agent Ben Hansen interviews Calvin Parker in Pascagoula, Mississippi, about the 1973 UFO abduction case.
- Calvin describes encountering three bulky, gray creatures while fishing with Charles “Charlie” Hickson, followed by an injection, paralysis, and an examination aboard a craft.
- After reporting the incident, the men were questioned at Keesler Air Force Base, but their accounts received no follow-up investigation.
- Calvin believes the beings came from another galaxy or planet rather than being fallen angels, and the experience left him unable to trust many people.
